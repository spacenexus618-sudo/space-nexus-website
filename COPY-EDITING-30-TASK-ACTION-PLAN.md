## Why Copy Editing Matters

**Without copy editing:**
- Confusing sentences lose readers in 5 seconds
- Generic claims don't build trust
- Features without benefits don't drive action
- Missing proof points don't convince buyers
- Unclear CTAs don't convert visitors

**With copy editing:**
- Clear copy keeps readers engaged
- Specific claims build credibility
- Benefits connect to customer needs
- Social proof drives conversions
- Risk reversals remove hesitation

## The Seven Sweeps Checklist

Use this for every page:

- [ ] **Sweep 1: Clarity** — Can the reader understand?
- [ ] **Sweep 2: Voice & Tone** — Is it consistent?
- [ ] **Sweep 3: So What** — Does every claim answer "why should I care?"
- [ ] **Sweep 4: Prove It** — Is every claim supported?
- [ ] **Sweep 5: Specificity** — Is it concrete enough?
- [ ] **Sweep 6: Heightened Emotion** — Does it make the reader feel something?
- [ ] **Sweep 7: Zero Risk** — Have we removed every barrier?
