# 5 Free Tool Ideas Built for SpaceNexus

## 1. M&A Due Diligence Readiness Grader

- **Tool Type:** Analyzer / Assessment

- **Target Audience:** Founders, CFOs, Corporate M&A Teams

- **Search Intent:** "How to prepare for due diligence", "M&A audit checklist"

- **How it Works:** Users answer 10-12 quick multi-choice questions about their company's financial records, legal compliance, IP, employee contracts, and cap table. The tool instantly calculates a Deal Readiness Score (0-100%) and flags missing critical documents.

- **Path to Product:** "Your cap table and IP assignments are missing 4 key documents. Click here to auto-index and store your files securely in a SpaceNexus AI Data Room."

## 2. Legacy VDR vs. Modern VDR Cost & Storage Estimator

- **Tool Type:** Calculator

- **Target Audience:** Investment Bankers, PE Analysts, Law Firms

- **Search Intent:** "Datasite pricing", "Intralinks cost calculator", "virtual data room cost comparison"

- **How it Works:** Users input deal size, number of buyers/bidders, expected duration (e.g., 3 months), and estimated document volume (pages or GB). The calculator displays a side-by-side cost breakdown comparing legacy per-page pricing against SpaceNexus transparent pricing.

- **Path to Product:** Highlighting potential cost savings (e.g., "Save $14,000 on per-page fees") with a direct call-to-action: "Lock in transparent pricing for your upcoming deal."

## 3. Free Web AI PII & Redaction Scanner (Mini-Tool)

- **Tool Type:** Interactive Micro-Tool / Tester

- **Target Audience:** M&A Lawyers, Paralegals, Corporate Development

- **Search Intent:** "Free PDF redaction tool", "auto redact SSN from legal contract"

- **How it Works:** A lightweight browser-based scanner where users upload a sample 5-page PDF. The tool automatically detects sensitive personal identifiable information (PII), signatures, and financial numbers, allowing users to apply redaction with one click. data-rooms.org

- **Path to Product:** "Redacted 5 pages in 2 seconds. Need to redact 10,000+ pages across your entire deal room? Activate SpaceNexus AI Redaction Studio."

## 4. Interactive Due Diligence Checklist Generator

- **Tool Type:** Generator / Resource Library

- **Target Audience:** Investment Bankers, Startup Founders, Legal Counsel

- **Search Intent:** "SaaS due diligence checklist XLS", "Real estate VDR folder structure"

- **How it Works:** Users select their Industry (e.g., SaaS, Life Sciences, Real Estate) and Transaction Type (Series A, Cross-Border M&A, Asset Sale). The generator outputs a tailored due diligence index and folder tree.

- **Path to Product:** Offers a download button: "Export to Excel" or "1-Click Import into a SpaceNexus Data Room" (pre-populating their virtual data room folder structure instantly).

## 5. Cap Table & M&A Payout Waterfall Calculator

- **Tool Type:** Financial Calculator

- **Target Audience:** Founders, Early Investors, Venture Capitalists

- **Search Intent:** "M&A payout waterfall model", "Series A cap table dilution calculator"

- **How it Works:** Users input current share classes, liquidation preferences (1x participating/non-participating), and exit valuation. The tool models payout distributions across common shareholders, preferred investors, and option pools.

- **Path to Product:** Prompts the user to save and share the waterfall output securely via a password-protected SpaceNexus deal link.
