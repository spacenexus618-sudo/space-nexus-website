## Homepage Analysis

### Current State

**Hero Section:**
- Headline: "AI-Powered Virtual Data Room Software for M&A, Fundraising & Legal Deals"
- CTA: "Talk to Founders" (primary) + "Explore Features" (secondary)
- Trust indicator: "Built by a team from: Fortune 500, Big 4 Consulting, Top Investment Banks"

**Issues Found:**

| Issue | Impact | Fix |
|-------|--------|-----|
| CTA says "Talk to Founders" — vague | Medium | Change to "Start Free Trial" or "See Demo" |
| No pricing mention on hero | Low | Add "Starting at $X/month" or "Free trial" |
| Trust indicator is generic | Low | Add specific company logos or names |
| No social proof numbers | Medium | Add "500+ enterprises" or "10,000+ documents processed" |

### Quick Wins (Implement Now)

1. **Change CTA from "Talk to Founders" to "Start Free Trial"**
   - Why: "Talk to Founders" sounds like a sales call
   - "Start Free Trial" is clearer and lower commitment
   - Expected impact: +15-25% CTA clicks

2. **Add pricing indicator on hero**
   - Why: Visitors want to know cost before clicking
   - Add: "Free 14-day trial · No credit card required"
   - Expected impact: +10-20% CTA clicks

3. **Add social proof numbers**
   - Why: Numbers build trust
   - Add: "500+ enterprises · 10,000+ documents · 99.9% uptime"
   - Expected impact: +10-15% trust

### High-Impact Changes (Prioritize)

1. **Replace "Talk to Founders" with "See Demo" or "Start Free Trial"**
   - Current: "Talk to Founders" → sounds like sales call
   - Better: "Start Free Trial" → clear action, low commitment
   - Best: "Start Free Trial — No Credit Card" → removes friction

2. **Add customer logos to hero section**
   - Current: Generic "Fortune 500, Big 4 Consulting"
   - Better: Show actual logos (even 3-4 recognizable ones)
   - Expected impact: +20-30% trust

3. **Add testimonial quote near CTA**
   - Current: No testimonial near hero CTA
   - Better: Add one quote with name + company
   - Expected impact: +15-25% conversions

### Test Ideas

| Test | Hypothesis | Metric |
|------|------------|--------|
| CTA copy | "Start Free Trial" outperforms "Talk to Founders" | CTA clicks |
| Hero headline | Specific benefit beats feature-focused | Time on page |
| Trust signals | Logos beat text | Bounce rate |

## Pricing Page Analysis

### Current State

**Hero Section:**
- Headline: "Simple, transparent pricing"
- Subhead: "No per-page fees. No per-user surprises. No hidden charges."
- Trust indicator: "Free 14-day trial · No credit card required"

**Pricing Cards:**
- Starter: "Contact Us"
- Professional: "Contact Us" (Most Popular)
- Enterprise: "Custom"

**Issues Found:**

| Issue | Impact | Fix |
|-------|--------|-----|
| All prices say "Contact Us" | High | Show actual prices or ranges |
| No recommended plan highlight | Medium | Add "Most Popular" badge (already there) |
| No ROI calculator | Medium | Add "Calculate your savings" tool |
| No comparison to competitors | Low | Add "Compare to Datasite" section |

### Quick Wins (Implement Now)

1. **Add price ranges or "Starting at"**
   - Current: All say "Contact Us"
   - Better: "Starting at $X/month" or "From $X/deal"
   - Why: Visitors want to know if they can afford it
   - Expected impact: +20-30% qualified leads

2. **Add "Most Popular" visual emphasis**
   - Current: Badge exists but card is only slightly larger
   - Better: Make Professional card more prominent
   - Expected impact: +10-15% plan selection

3. **Add ROI calculator**
   - Current: None
   - Better: "See how much you save vs Datasite"
   - Expected impact: +25-40% engagement

### High-Impact Changes (Prioritize)

1. **Show actual prices (or ranges)**
   - Current: "Contact Us" for all plans
   - Better: "Starter: $X/deal · Professional: $X/month · Enterprise: Custom"
   - Why: Hidden prices scare away visitors
   - Expected impact: +30-50% qualified leads

2. **Add competitor comparison table**
   - Current: Only compares SpaceNexus plans
   - Better: Add "SpaceNexus vs Datasite vs Intralinks" column
   - Expected impact: +20-30% conversions

3. **Add "Which plan is right for me?" section**
   - Current: None
   - Better: Quiz or recommendation engine
   - Expected impact: +15-25% plan selection

### Test Ideas

| Test | Hypothesis | Metric |
|------|------------|--------|
| Price display | Showing prices outperforms "Contact Us" | Qualified leads |
| Plan highlighting | Emphasizing "Most Popular" increases selection | Plan distribution |
| ROI calculator | Adding calculator increases engagement | Time on page |
